# EURUSD M15 — October 2026 Backtest

Run: https://github.com/negahdarfar/aron-mt5-demo-trader/actions/runs/43720189191

- Data: Dukascopy EURUSD bid candles, M15
- Trade window: 2026-10-01 00:00 UTC through 2026-11-01 00:00 UTC (exclusive)
- Warm-up data begins 2026-09-01; trades are filtered to October only
- Starting balance: 1,000 (simulation units)
- Risk settings: project defaults
- All results are offline research, not broker execution

## Risk flags

- EMA/RSI hit both the daily-loss and max-drawdown guardrails in both cases.
- SMC hit the daily-loss guardrail in both cases; it did not hit max drawdown in the zero-cost case, but did in the cost-sensitivity case.
- No position remained open at the end of the test.

## Interpretation

Neither strategy demonstrates a robust positive edge after the tested transaction-cost assumption. EMA/RSI's small zero-cost gain turns negative with costs. SMC's small zero-cost gain turns sharply negative with costs. Do not use these results to authorize live trading.

## Critical sizing and cost limitation

The backtest currently uses `value_per_price_unit_per_lot = 1.0` as a placeholder. This is not calibrated to Aron Groups' EURUSD contract specification or the account currency. Therefore the displayed P/L and ending-equity amounts are simulation outputs, not reliable USD forecasts. Commission, swap, margin, lot step/minimum volume, account-currency conversion, and broker-specific execution are not modeled. The 1.5-pip spread/slippage case is hypothetical and is not a verified Aron Groups quote.

## Decision

Keep the project in research/demo-only mode. Before interpreting monetary results, calibrate pip/tick value and contract sizing against the exact Aron Groups MT5 EURUSD symbol specification, then rerun this same October-only test.
